Who we are

Bryant Vending Services is a trading name of Bryant Group Holdings Ltd (company no. 13671131), registered office 32 Heaton Road, Bournemouth, BH10 5HW. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

What we collect and why

This website has no forms, cookies, tracking or analytics. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we use your name, contact details and what you tell us about your location to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to agree and manage a vending arrangement. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or taking steps towards a contract at your request.

We may email businesses whose contact details are publicly listed to ask whether vending could be useful to them. Every such email lets you opt out, and we will not contact you again if you ask us not to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is held with our email and phone providers, and shared with others only where needed to provide the service or where the law requires. The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Records for agreed arrangements are kept for as long as the arrangement runs and then for up to 6 years for legal and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, including asking us to stop contacting you. Email info@bryantvending.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
